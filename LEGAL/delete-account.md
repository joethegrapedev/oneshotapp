# Delete Your Account & Data

One Shot lets you delete your account and all associated data at any time,
directly inside the app. This page explains how, and what is removed.

## How to delete your account in the app

1. Open **One Shot**.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your journal entries (private and shared).
- Your account and authentication identity (including a linked email, if you
  added one).
- Your matches, and the reports and blocks you created.
- Your profile.

**Retention:** Minimal moderation records may be retained where required for
safety, abuse-prevention, or legal compliance, as described in our
[Privacy Policy](/privacy). Copies of an entry you previously chose to share
anonymously may persist where they can no longer be linked back to you.
